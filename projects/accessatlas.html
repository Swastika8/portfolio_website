AccessAtlas

Crowdsourced accessibility information for public places.

Finding out before you go

I built AccessAtlas to make questions about step-free entrances, elevators, and accessible washrooms easier to answer before a visit.

The map centers on individual facilities and community observations. Someone can look up a place, filter by the feature they need, and contribute an observation when the information changes.

Handling uncertainty

Accessibility details can change over time. The data model keeps places, facilities, and reports separate so a facility can have a history of observations. Missing information is recorded as unknown, and the reporting workflow is designed to support confirmation and review.
